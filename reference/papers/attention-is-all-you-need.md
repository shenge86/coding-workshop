## In one sentence

Introduces the **transformer**, an architecture that processes sequences (like text) using a mechanism called *self-attention* rather than the recurrent or convolutional structures that came before. Every modern large language model — GPT, Claude, Llama, Gemini, all of them — is a descendant of this paper.

## Why it matters for someone using AI

- This is the architecture under the hood of essentially every chatbot you've used.
- The paper's title is half a joke and half a thesis: previous architectures had attention as a *helper*; this one has attention as the *whole game*.
- Knowing the word "transformer" comes from here, not from the toy line, helps when you're reading anything else in the field.
