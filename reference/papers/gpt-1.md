## In one sentence

Showed that you could **pretrain** a transformer on a lot of unlabeled text, then **fine-tune** it on a specific task, and beat models that were custom-built for that task. This two-step recipe became the template for everything that followed.

## Why it matters for someone using AI

- This is the moment "general-purpose language model" became a viable idea.
- The output is, by modern standards, near-nonsense for anything beyond short snippets — but the *recipe* worked, and that recipe got scaled up. This is the floor of the trajectory.
