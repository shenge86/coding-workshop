## In one sentence

Scaled the same recipe to 175B parameters and demonstrated **in-context learning**: give the model a handful of examples in the prompt, and it will pattern-match to do the task — without any weight updates.

## Why it matters for someone using AI

- This is the paper that "broke containment" — it's what kicked off the wave of public AI products that followed.
- The phrase **few-shot learning** comes from here. So does the modern intuition that "if it's not working, try giving it examples."
- It's the first time the model felt qualitatively *different* — not just better at completion, but able to follow instructions, write code, translate, summarize, all from the same weights.
