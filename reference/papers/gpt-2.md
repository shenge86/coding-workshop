## In one sentence

Made the model bigger (1.5B parameters) and the training data more diverse, and discovered that a sufficiently large language model trained only to predict the next token can do many tasks **with no fine-tuning at all** — just by being prompted.

## Why it matters for someone using AI

- This is where the modern idea of **prompting** starts. You ask, it answers. You frame the task in the input, the model handles it from context.
- It's also the moment OpenAI hesitated to release a model on safety grounds — the first time the broader public noticed that this technology might be powerful enough to need thinking about.
- Outputs are noticeably more coherent than GPT-1, but still wander, hallucinate, and contradict themselves. You can feel the trajectory bending upward.
